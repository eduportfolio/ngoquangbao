Scientific Research

Research on Microplastics & Trace Metals in Aquatic Environments

This study evaluates the biofilm-mediated interactions between microplastics (MPs) and trace metals in aquatic ecosystems. Upon entering the environment, microplastic surfaces are rapidly colonized by microorganisms, forming biofilms that significantly alter surface properties such as roughness, charge, and functional groups (carboxyl, hydroxyl, phosphoryl, etc.). The findings indicate that biofilm-coated microplastics act not only as a "sink" that adsorbs heavy metals through electrostatic attraction or surface complexation, but can also serve as a secondary pollution source when environmental conditions or organism digestive systems induce metal desorption.

Research on Organic Waste Bioconversion using Black Soldier Fly Larvae

An empirical study conducted across 30 households in Ninh Binh, Vietnam, evaluated the efficiency of household organic waste treatment using Black Soldier Fly Larvae (BSFL; Hermetia illucens). The project compared three substrate groups: fruit/vegetable waste (Group A), animal-based food waste (Group B), and mixed organic waste (Group C). Results demonstrated that mixed waste (Group C) achieved the highest waste reduction efficiency (83.54 ± 0.79% on a dry matter basis). Meanwhile, animal-derived waste (Group B) yielded the highest total fresh biomass (439.6 ± 14.4 g/box), maximum average larva length (2.90 ± 0.09 cm), and the highest soluble protein content extracted via NaOH (47.79 ± 1.04% DM).

Antimicrobial Efficacy of Vetiver Root Extracts & Sanitizer Applications

An independent research project investigating the zone of inhibition and minimal inhibitory concentration (MIC) of Chrysopogon zizanioides (Vetiver) root extracts against common pathogenic microorganisms. By optimizing the solvent extraction process, the study successfully evaluates the bio-efficacy of volatile essential oils and phytochemicals, paving the way for developing an organic, eco-friendly, and non-toxic herbal sanitizer formulation.
