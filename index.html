Research & Innovation

Antimicrobial Efficacy of Vetiver Root Extracts & Sanitizer Applications

An independent research project investigating the zone of inhibition and minimal inhibitory concentration (MIC) of Chrysopogon zizanioides (Vetiver) root extracts against common pathogenic microorganisms. By optimizing the solvent extraction process, the study successfully evaluates the bio-efficacy of volatile essential oils and phytochemicals, paving the way for developing an organic, eco-friendly, and non-toxic herbal sanitizer formulation.
